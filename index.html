Fewest buses, every time

Pick two stops. The planner finds the journey with the fewest boardings, shows where to change, and re-plans instantly when you switch a route off.

Route board

Tap a route to close or reopen it. The journey re-plans on every change.

Why this is the minimum

The search moves one bus at a time, not one stop. Ring 1 holds every route that stops at your origin; ring 2 holds every new route you can switch to from those, and so on. The first ring that touches the destination is the answer, so no journey with fewer buses can exist.

Fewest buses vs fewest stops

A commuter cares about how many times they board, not how many stops pass by. The shortest path by stops can force extra changes; this planner never does.

Network

Sample networks are illustrative. Riding between any two stops on a route counts as one bus. Search cost is linear in total stop appearances (≤ 40,000).
